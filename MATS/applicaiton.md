Long terms career goal
In long term my goal is to contribute fundamentally to the theoretical understanding of AI safety, with a specific focus on developing robust and interpretable large language and vision-language models. With my experience in Visual Reasoning Models, multi-modal deep learning, RAG pipelines, and model optimization, I intend pursue a career in academic research, where I can explore novel approaches to ensure AI systems are aligned with human values and intentions. I aspire to develop theoretical frameworks and practical methodologies for areas such as corrigibility, inner alignment, and deceptive alignment, particularly within the context of  VLM and LLM architectures. Ultimately, I would like to see my research playing a critical role in laying the intellectual groundwork necessary for the safe development and deployment of artificial general intelligence.
