Long terms career goal
In long term my goal is to contribute fundamentally to the theoretical understanding of AI safety, with a specific focus on developing robust and interpretable large language and vision-language models. With my experience in Visual Reasoning Models, multi-modal deep learning, RAG pipelines, and model optimization, I intend pursue a career in academic research, where I can explore novel approaches to ensure AI systems are aligned with human values and intentions. I aspire to develop theoretical frameworks and practical methodologies for areas such as corrigibility, inner alignment, and deceptive alignment, particularly within the context of  VLM and LLM architectures. Ultimately, I would like to see my research playing a critical role in laying the intellectual groundwork necessary for the safe development and deployment of artificial general intelligence.


While I haven't specifically conducted research or project titled "AI Safety," my practical experience in building, optimizing, and deploying complex AI systems, coupled with my understanding of their internal workings and potential failure modes, directly contributes to the foundational knowledge required for AI safety research. My long-term goal to contribute to the theoretical understanding of AI safety  reflects my strong commitment to this critical field.
My current role as a Pre Doc Research Fellow at IIT Hyderabad focuses on Spatial Feature Reasoning using VLMs, which involves understanding spatial relationships in images and text. This kind of research into how AI models perceive and interpret complex, real-world information is crucial for ensuring their safe and predictable behavior. Misinterpretations or flawed reasoning can lead to unsafe outputs, and addressing these issues is a core part of AI safety.
